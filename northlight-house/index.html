WELCOME TO NORTHLIGHT HOUSE

Explore the architecture.

This is a completely new Three.js building inspired by the Meridian Court idea, with its own design, individually furnished flats and a rooftop garden.

Architectural view
Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, select a floor on the left. The cutaway button lets you peek inside.
Walk inside
Use W A S D to walk, move your mouse to look, Shift to run and E to open a nearby door. Use the floor buttons to jump directly to another level.
Touch devices
Use architectural view to rotate and inspect floors. In walking mode, use the on-screen arrows and drag the 3D scene to look. Tap the interaction prompt to use furniture and light switches.
